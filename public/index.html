How it works

  1. An auditor publishes a signed manifest

    Who they are, how they examine, their terms and prices — all signed with their key. Anyone can become an auditor in the app, with an Onym phrase.

  2. They examine exact bytes and sign a verdict

    The attestation is bound to the hash of the code or service manifest it examined, and names the findings, who paid and every relationship. The auditor's register is anchored in the Stellar blockchain.

  3. Anyone checks it themselves

    Each attestation's page verifies the signatures in your browser, and the Onym apps show a service's audit status through the Discovery catalog.
