Anyone can take the seat

Become an auditor of the Onym network

Examine anything in the network — a repository at a commit, a running courier, notary, storage, moderation or backup service, a Discovery catalog, an app build — and sign what you found. Your key stays in this browser; the hub only publishes what you signed.

  1. Pick a name. Your browser creates your Ed25519 key; you download a backup of it.
  2. Examine something. Findings quote the exact bytes they rest on; Discovery catalogs get the full 42-check suite.
  3. Sign and publish. Your attestations appear on your own page, verified in every reader's browser.

The hub holds only a delegated key that keeps your status list fresh; it cannot sign, change, or revoke anything for you. You can take your whole tree and host it elsewhere at any time.
