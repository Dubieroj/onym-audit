Your auditor identity

An auditor is an Onym identity of its own: twelve words, from which your auditor key and Stellar account are derived exactly as the Onym apps derive them. Add the same words to the Onym app as a second account and it shows the same address.

Never enter the phrase of your main Onym account here. That phrase is your whole identity — messages, groups and keys — and an auditor key is public: it would link your auditing to your conversations. Use a phrase made for auditing only.

The phrase never leaves this page and is not stored: the browser keeps only keys it cannot export. Nothing is written to the Stellar network and no funds are needed.
