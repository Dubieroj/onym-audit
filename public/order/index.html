Order an audit

Choose an auditor and one of their signed offers, point at exact bytes, and sign the order in your browser. The price and the disclosure terms are fixed before anyone looks — nothing you pay can change the result.

  1. You pick an auditor and an offer and fill in the form. Your browser creates an Ed25519 key and signs the order with it — as the component’s owner and as the paying party.
  2. The order waits in that auditor’s queue. Before accepting it, the auditor checks it against their independence policy.
  3. The auditor examines exactly the bytes the order pins and sends you the findings first.
  4. A signed attestation is published. With your key you can post a signed reply beside it, and nobody can edit that reply.
