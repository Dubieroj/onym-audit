# Liability terms

Auditor: onym audit (`onym:component:onym-audit`). Version 1, 24 September 2026.

An attestation is professional judgment under a named, versioned
methodology, about exact bytes, at a point in time (Onym Audit.md §8.9).

- **What the auditor answers for:** that the published methodology was
  applied as written to the bytes the attestation names, that the scope and
  exclusions are complete, that the sponsor and every relationship are
  stated, and that the attestation is revoked when the auditor no longer
  stands behind it — including after compromise of the auditor key.
- **What it does not answer for:** anything outside the stated scope or
  listed in `exclusions`; any other revision, build, fork, or deployment;
  the subject's behaviour after the examination; and whether a relying
  party's decision to rely was right. An attestation is not a
  certification, a licence, or a warranty.
- **Financial liability** is limited to the fees paid for the engagement.
  Attestations issued `pro-bono` or unsolicited carry no financial
  liability.
- **Errors** are corrected by supersession or revocation with a stated
  reason, never by editing a published attestation.
