# Independence policy

Auditor: Dimitrii (`onym:component:dimitrii-audit`). Version 1, 24 September 2026.

This policy is pinned by digest in the auditor manifest. It binds every
attestation issued under that manifest (Onym Audit.md §8.4).

## Ownership and operation

The auditor owns, operates, and is paid by no Onym component and no
component's operator. The auditor does not operate a Discovery catalog.
Any future ownership, operation, employment, or payment relationship with a
subject is declared in the `relationships` field of every attestation
about that subject, and the auditor recuses from subjects it owns,
operates, or competes with unless the sponsor accepts the disclosed
conflict in a signed order.

## Declared standing relationship

The auditor built this seat as a participant in **Sobor 2026**, a contest
in Bar, Montenegro (21–25 September 2026) whose organizer is the maintainer
of the Onym reference components, and whose €1,000 prize fund that
organizer raised. The organizer does not judge the contest; an independent
arbiter does. Every attestation about an Onym-operated component states
this relationship. It is disclosed so relying parties can discount for it —
including the possibility that an adverse finding is read as contest
positioning.

## Fees and verdicts

Fees are verdict-independent (Audit.md §5.3.1). The auditor accepts no
payment, gift, or advantage from a subject in connection with an
unsolicited attestation, and never offers to withhold, soften, or delay an
attestation for payment (§5.7).

## Recusal

The auditor refuses an engagement when it cannot state every relationship
it has with the subject, or when the sponsor asks for anything that would
make the result contingent on payment.
